DAILY SCHEDULER
=========================
A database application to manage and track daily assignments efficiently, ensuring timely communication between departments and the supervisor.

Key Features:
- Register and monitor daily tasks with due dates and completion status.
- Track the exact date notifications were received from departments to prevent scheduling conflicts.
- Generate reports highlighting pending and upcoming assignments.
- Integrate with MS Outlook to send daily schedules automatically to the supervisor.

Impact / Benefits:
-Kept supervisors informed and allowed proactive planning for incoming assignments.
-Identified departments frequently missing notifications, enabling corrective actions.
-Provided historical data to anticipate high-traffic days and manage workload.
-Reduced conflicts and improved accountability between departments.

BOOKING ROOMS
=========================
A Microsoft Access application to manage and automate room bookings for company meetings, replacing the error-prone phone/email procedure.

Key Features / Benefits:
-Hosted in a shared folder with controlled access for employees.
-Employees submit booking requests through a form; required fields are validated with informative messages.
-Automatically generates a report of the request and emails it to the meeting-room administrator.
-Preview and send buttons allow users to review or dispatch requests without extra steps.
-Hovering or tabbing on inputs provides guidance on required fields.
-Ensures accurate data submission, avoiding missing or incomplete booking requests.
-Centralizes all booking requests for easier monitoring and conflict resolution.

Impact:
-Eliminated scheduling conflicts and missed requests from the old phone/email system.
-Enabled the administrator to manage room bookings efficiently.
-Ensured completeness and correctness of submitted requests.
-Streamlined department workflow and improved accountability.


HSE DATABASE
=========================
A unified Microsoft Access database providing comprehensive employee and fleet management information, supporting the Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) department.

Key Features / Benefits:
-Maintain complete records of all employees, including job role, department, contact info, and location.
-Track employee driving skills, training dates, scores, and trainer comments.
-Manage company car assignments, recording vehicle details (brand, CC, color, leasing info, features, mileage, purchase date) and assigned drivers.
-Allocate and monitor parking spaces across multiple buildings, correlating them with assigned vehicles and employees.
-Provide a consolidated overview of each employee by integrating roadsafety, safetracks, and accident data.
-Evaluate annual road safety performance for each driver with multi-row assessments and manager/HSE feedback.
-Record and categorize driving accidents, including driver, date, description, and HSE classification.
-Facilitate searching and reporting across all domains for quick, accurate access to information.

Impact:
-Centralized HSE and employee data for streamlined monitoring and compliance.
-Enhanced visibility of driver performance, vehicle assignments, and parking management.
-Supported risk assessment, safety tracking, and incident analysis effectively.
-Reduced manual tracking errors and improved departmental efficiency.
